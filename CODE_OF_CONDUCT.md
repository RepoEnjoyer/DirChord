# Community code of conduct

DirChord welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels. Participation should remain respectful, specific, and focused on improving the project.

Expected behavior includes giving actionable feedback, assuming good faith while still reviewing claims carefully, respecting privacy, and making space for people to ask basic questions. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate exposure of private information, and repeated disruptive behavior are not accepted.

Project maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's Security tab when public discussion would expose someone or worsen the situation.
